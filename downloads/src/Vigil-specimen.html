Fewer things, measured faster.

Product strategy is mostly the discipline of choosing what not to build. The teams that win are rarely the ones with the longest roadmap; they are the ones that ship fewer things, measure them faster, and quit the wrong bets early. A good text face should support that kind of reading — steady at sixteen pixels, unremarkable in the best sense, and quietly warm when it is finally set large.
