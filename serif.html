Vigil Serif

A serif companion to Vigil, in preview. Derived from Vollkorn by Friedrich Althausen and reproportioned to sit beside the sans: the same cap height, the same x-height, the same line box, so the two can share a page. Working name. Free under the SIL Open Font License when it ships.

Serif at 300 and sans at 400 share a stem; at 400 the serif is heavier.

Cap height, x-height and line box match exactly. At the same nominal weight the serif's stems are heavier than the sans's, so the stem-matched pairing is serif 300 beside sans 400.
