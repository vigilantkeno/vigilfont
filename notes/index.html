Notes

What font is that? Field notes on the lettering in public life — road signs, license plates, logos, jerseys, the Constitution — and on the geometric and rounded sans serifs Vigil comes from. Every claim is sourced, and the answer is often a correction to the one everyone repeats.

  1. 2026-09-21The airport font is Frutiger — and Heathrow ran a serifDrawn for Charles de Gaulle, released in 1976. Heathrow used a custom serif for a decade, and the testing backed it.
  2. 2026-09-21The baseball jersey font isn’t a fontThe rulebook legislates six inches of height and nothing else. The letters are cut from cloth, not typed.
  3. 2026-09-19The NFL font is three different answersEndzone for the brand, Tackle for the network, thirty-two numeral sets for the jerseys. The shield isn’t type at all.
  4. 2026-09-19The Constitution isn’t written in a fontA clerk, a quill and a weekend. The headings really are blackletter; the body really isn’t.
  5. 2026-09-18Why a serif, and why VollkornPairing someone else’s serif is arithmetic. What it takes to make one measure the same as the sans.
  6. 2026-09-14There is no VHS fontThe logo is Lee, 1976. The on-screen text was a chip, not a typeface. The look is mostly signal damage.
  7. 2026-09-14Fonts similar to Avenir — and what they missAvenir broke the geometry on purpose. Substitute it with a strict geometric sans and you undo the fix.
  8. 2026-09-14The Spotify font is Spotify MixSpotify Mix, built by Dinamo in 2024. It replaced Circular — it is not a custom cut of it.
  9. 2026-09-14Fonts like Futura: what actually comes closeJost, Poppins and League Spartan are free. Century Gothic is a copy of a copy.

  11. 2026-09-09The New York subway font isn’t the one you thinkThe signs are Helvetica. The 1970 manual wasn’t. Both are still down there.

  13. 2026-09-07The Coca-Cola logo isn’t a fontA bookkeeper’s handwriting from 1886, in the penmanship style he already used at work.
  14. 2026-09-06What font is easiest to read?There is no single easiest font. Size, spacing and line length decide more than the typeface does.
